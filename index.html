Deployment & Execution

How to Run & Deploy the Android Application

Choose the method that works best for your workflow:

🌐

1. Instant Browser Emulator

Recommended for quick review & testing

Stream the complete native Android app live in your web browser with zero installation. Works on any laptop or desktop.

Open Browser Emulator
💻

2. Android Studio (Full Build)

For developers and release engineering

  1. Clone this GitHub repository to your computer.
  2. Open the folder in Android Studio Ladybug / Koala.
  3. Sync Gradle and press Run ▶ or execute:
./gradlew assembleDebug
Output: app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk
📱

3. Install on Android Phone

For field officers on duty

In Google AI Studio, click the project menu at top right and choose Download APK. Transfer the .apk to your Android smartphone or tablet and tap to install.

Prerequisites:

• Android 8.0 (API 26) or higher

• Enable "Install Unknown Apps" in Settings

Key Capabilities Built-in

⚡
Spot & Self Inspections

On-demand field inspections with GPS coordinates, accuracy thresholds, and photo evidence validation.

📄
Word & Excel Dossiers

Complete official Microsoft Word (.docx) and Excel (.xlsx) dossiers with in-app preview before download.

⚠️
Compliance Tracking

Assign action officers, set closure deadlines, log rectified photo proofs, and monitor overdue compliances.

🔄
Dual-Role Switcher

Seamlessly toggle between Field Officer and Admin/HQ reviewer modes with role-based dashboard metrics.
